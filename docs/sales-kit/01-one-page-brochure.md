# SOLVA HR

## Smart Payroll & HR Management Platform for Modern Businesses

### Simplify Payroll. Empower HR. Improve Workforce Operations.

Solva HR is a modern cloud-based payroll and HR management system built for businesses in Kenya and beyond. It helps organizations automate payroll, manage staff records, streamline approvals, improve employee experience, and generate statutory reports in one secure platform.

---

## Key Features

### Payroll Management

- 15th and month-end payroll processing
- Payslip generation and ESS access
- PAYE, SHIF, NSSF, HELB, and Housing Levy reports
- Net-to-MPESA exports
- Payroll approvals and audit trails
- Salary reviews and payroll adjustments

### Employee Self Service (ESS)

- Download payslips
- Apply leave
- View leave balances
- Access shifts and rosters
- View performance status
- Access contracts and HR letters

### Leave & Attendance

- Annual leave tracking
- Off-day requests
- Attendance monitoring
- Shift roster management
- Absenteeism deduction workflows

### Performance Management

- KPI tracking
- Appraisals and reviews
- Promotion cases
- Talent matrix
- Supervisor and GM scoring

### HR Operations

- Employee records management
- HR letters and contracts
- Disciplinary workflows
- Staff onboarding and offboarding
- Employee relations tracking

### Reports & Compliance

- Wagebill reports
- Posting lists
- Payroll registers
- Statutory exports
- Executive dashboards
- Audit logs
