## Slide 2 — The Problem

# HR and Payroll Operations Need Modernization

- Many organizations still depend on spreadsheets, paper forms, fragmented tools, and manual approvals.
- Payroll, leave, performance, and reporting often sit in disconnected systems.
- The result is slower operations, weaker visibility, compliance exposure, and avoidable payroll errors.

---

## Slide 3 — The Solution

# Meet Solva HR

- Solva HR is a cloud-based HR and payroll platform built for African business operations.
- It helps organizations run payroll accurately, manage employees digitally, automate approvals, track leave and attendance, and improve workforce accountability.
- It combines operational control, employee self-service, and management visibility in one platform.

---

## Slide 4 — Differentiation

# Why Solva HR Stands Out

- African payroll first: supports PAYE, SHIF, NSSF, HELB, Housing Levy, and Net-to-Bank workflows.
- One connected operating system for HR, payroll, approvals, attendance, performance, and reporting.
- Practical, localized, and scalable for growing organizations.
- Executive visibility through real-time dashboards, approval tracking, and workforce insights.

---

## Slide 5 — Modules

# What Solva HR Covers

- Core HR: employee records, departments, branches, organization structure, and document management.
- Payroll: processing, approvals, payslips, validations, statutory reporting, wagebill, and bank exports.
- Leave and Attendance: leave requests, off days, attendance tracking, shift scheduling, and roster uploads.
- Performance: KPIs, goals, appraisals, PIPs, promotions, succession planning, and talent tracking.
- ESS: payslips, leave, performance, complaints, requests, and notifications.

---

## Slide 6 — Smart Operations

# Built for Modern Workforce Operations

- AI-assisted support for payroll insights, KPI guidance, anomaly flags, and workforce summaries.
- Centralized approvals inbox for leave, payroll, promotions, salary changes, and performance decisions.
- Role-based access so every user sees only what is relevant to them.
- A more controlled and accountable way to run people operations.

## Slide 8 — Payroll & Compliance

# Payroll Accuracy and Compliance

- Supports PAYE, SHIF, NSSF, HELB, Housing Levy, Net-to-Bank, payroll registers, wagebill, and earnings and deductions reports.
- Payroll controls include validations, approvals, audit trails, locked periods, reconciliation summaries, and export history.
- Designed to improve compliance confidence while reducing payroll processing risk.

## Slide 9 — Performance

# Performance That Supports Real Decisions

- Solva HR supports KPI setting, self-appraisals, supervisor reviews, GM calibration, quarterly evaluations, work plans, and improvement plans.
- Performance links naturally to promotions, rewards, training, probation follow-up, and payroll-impact decisions.
- This makes performance management practical, measurable, and decision-ready.

## Slide 10 — Employee Experience

# A Better Employee Experience

- Employees can download payslips, request leave, request off days, view shifts, track KPIs, monitor performance, receive notices, submit complaints, and access documents.

- Staff get clarity while management gets better records and accountability.

## Slide 11 — Executive View

## Slide 12 — Foundations
