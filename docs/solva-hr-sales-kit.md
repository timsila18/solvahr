## Quick Sales Summary

### 30-Second Pitch

Solva HR is a modern cloud-based HR and payroll platform built for businesses that want to simplify payroll, improve staff management, centralize approvals, and give employees a better self-service experience.

### 60-Second Pitch

Solva HR helps organizations move away from spreadsheets, paper approvals, and disconnected HR processes. It brings payroll, employee records, leave, performance, reporting, approvals, ESS, and compliance into one secure platform. The system is designed for Kenyan business operations while still feeling modern, premium, and enterprise-ready.

### Core Selling Angles

- Kenyan payroll and compliance focused
- Role-based approvals and accountability
- Employee Self Service for staff convenience
- Multi-branch support
- Professional payroll and HR reports
- Strong operational control for HR, payroll, supervisors, and management
